How to read it: the page shows the video at exactly one video pixel per screen pixel. Step back, or look from across the room. If you can read WRONG in a panel, the browser put the chroma somewhere other than where the file says; if the panel looks like a flat field, it got it right. Close up the word is always faintly visible as a change of texture; judge it from a distance. First check the patch at the top right of the video: its two halves must look equally bright from a distance, or the picture is being scaled and the result means nothing. Keys: ←/→ previous/next file, 1/2 first panel blank/word, 3/4 second panel blank/word.
